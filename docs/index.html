Shop for Mom

A single-player 2D resource management game where you race against store closing time while keeping your mischievous sister in check.

Game Description

It is 9:58pm and the grocery store closes at 10:00pm. You procrastinated your mom's shopping list all day and now you are in a last-minute rush with your chaotic little sister. She jumps out of the cart to grab wrong items and even removes needed ones, creating constant cart management pressure while time runs out.

Your goal is to complete the list exactly: no missing required items and no extra items. Success depends on quick movement, careful item tracking, and recovering from your sister's interruptions before the store closes.

How to Play

  • Move: W A S D
  • Pick up glowing items: P
  • Drop items: F
  • Correct items: On your shopping list
  • Incorrect items: All other items (minus points)

Because the game is 2D and aisle-based, movement is up/down/left/right with horizontal limits in narrow aisles.
